Privacy Policy

Your finances are personal. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

01

Data We Collect

We only collect the information needed to run Spendly for you:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage data — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the service, including to:

  • Display your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account from unauthorised access.
  • Send essential account-related messages, such as password resets.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers, and passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are permanently removed.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers — for example, to host the application or deliver emails. These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing or analytics.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to access, correct, or delete your data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all requests within a few working days.
